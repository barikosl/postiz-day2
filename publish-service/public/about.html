Day2 Social Publisher

By Day2 AI, Israel. Short videos for small local businesses, posted to the business's own TikTok account, by the business owner.

Who it is for

Small service businesses in Israel (plumbers, electricians, renovators, movers and similar) that film their work on their phone but have no time to edit and post. Each business has its own TikTok account and stays its owner. Day2 AI sells this as a monthly service. The Hebrew service page is day2-ai.com/services/social-video.

How it works

  1. The owner connects their own TikTok account once, with TikTok Login Kit. They see TikTok's consent screen and can remove access any time in TikTok settings.
  2. The owner sends raw footage of their own work (on WhatsApp). Day2 edits it into a short vertical video and suggests a caption. Only the business's original footage is used. Nothing is copied from other platforms.
  3. The owner reviews and posts. They get a private link to a "Post to TikTok" page for their account. It shows their TikTok nickname and avatar, a playable preview and an editable caption. They choose who can see the video (no default; only the options TikTok allows for their account) and whether to allow comments, Duet and Stitch (all off by default). They can disclose commercial content (Your brand / Branded content), and they see TikTok's Music Usage Confirmation and Branded Content Policy. The video is sent to TikTok only when the owner presses "Post to TikTok".
  4. The page shows TikTok's processing status and a link to the post once it is live.

Day2 never chooses the privacy setting and never posts on the owner's behalf. Owners who prefer to finish in the TikTok app can instead get the video as a draft in their TikTok inbox.

TikTok data we store

The account's access and refresh tokens, encrypted with AES-256-GCM before they are written to our database (the key is kept separately from the database and its backups; the database runs on a private network and is not exposed to the internet), the account's open_id, display name and avatar, and the publish_id / share URL of each post. Nothing returned by creator_info is stored; it is fetched fresh every time the posting page opens. Owners can disconnect at any time.
